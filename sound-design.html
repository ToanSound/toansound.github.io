Freelance sound designer for film, ads and video

Your edit is locked, but it still feels flat. As a freelance sound designer, I build the sound around your picture: impacts, whooshes, textures, ambiences and the small details that make a cut feel real. Every sound is synced to the frame and mixed to sit with your music and dialogue. Toan Sound is based in Paris and works with directors, editors, agencies and creators worldwide, fully remotely.

What the sound design includes

  • Sound effects designed for your picture: hits, transitions, whooshes, motion and interface sounds
  • Ambiences and room tone that place the viewer in the scene
  • Layered textures built from recorded sources, synths and processing, not stock presets
  • A clean stereo mix with your music and voice-over
  • WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound; stems as an option

Built from scratch, like SCRATCH

SCRATCH is my sound design series: I mute a scene and rebuild every sound with everyday objects, a balloon, a hair dryer, my voice. It is the same method I bring to client work: real sources, recorded and shaped until they fit the picture. For example, see how the Godzilla roar was built from a balloon. Watch SCRATCH

On commercial work, I have handled sound design, editing and mix for Rolex ("Reach for the Crown", the launch film with Coco Gauff), ESCP Business School and Pascal le Grand Frais. See selected work

Sound design options

  • Up to 30 s of picture · typical turnaround: 4 working days
  • Up to 60 s · typical turnaround: 5 working days
  • Up to 2 min · typical turnaround: 7 working days
  • Up to 5 min · typical turnaround: 10 working days
  • Longer projects: quote on request

Two rounds of revisions and the stereo mix are included. Rush delivery and stems are available as options. Long foley or ambience work is quoted separately (foley).

How a sound design project works

  1. Send your picture and a short brief. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included unless stated otherwise.
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Toan Sound is based in Paris, and most projects are done entirely online: you send the picture by link, you get the files back the same way.

What do I need to send?

A locked picture (MP4 or MOV), your music and voice-over if you have them, and a short brief. References from films or ads you like help a lot.

Can you mix the dialogue and music too?

Yes, the stereo mix is included. For long films or heavy dialogue work, see sound mixing for short films.

Tell me about your edit: length, deadline, references. You get a fixed quote in writing before any work starts.
