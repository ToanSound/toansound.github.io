Freelance sound design for video games

Players feel a game through its sound: a jump that lands, a menu that clicks, a world that breathes. I design custom sound effects, ambiences and interface sounds for indie games and prototypes, delivered as clean, consistently named files, ready to use in your game.

What I can design for your game

  • Action sounds: footsteps, hits, abilities, pickups, doors, vehicles
  • UI and feedback sounds: menus, notifications, rewards, errors
  • Ambiences and loops for each level or area
  • Several variations of each sound, so repeated actions never sound copy-pasted
  • Sound design and mix for your game trailer (see details)

Sounds with their own identity

Stock libraries make games sound alike. In my SCRATCH series, every sound is rebuilt from recorded everyday objects, then shaped until it fits the picture. Your game gets the same treatment: sounds designed for its world, not picked from a catalogue. Listen to the Zelda chest sound, rebuilt from scratch. Watch SCRATCH

How the files are delivered

  • WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz by default, other sample rates or formats on request
  • One file per sound, with clear names and a sound list
  • Loops cut to loop seamlessly

Pricing for game sound design

Every game is different, so game audio is quoted from your sound list. Send the list, a build or a video capture, and you get a fixed price and a deadline in writing before any work starts. For a game trailer, sound design starts for up to 30 seconds.

How a game sound project works

  1. Send a build, a capture or your sound list. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included unless stated otherwise.
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

Do I need a finished build?

No. A gameplay capture, a few screenshots with a list of the sounds you need, or a design document are enough to quote.

Can you make music for the game too?

Yes, see original music. Short UI sound sets can also be designed as a coherent family, like app sounds.

Tell me about your game: genre, style, the sounds you need and your deadline.
