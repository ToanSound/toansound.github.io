Foley artist for hire, for film and animation

Foley is what makes a picture feel physical: the footsteps, the keys, the jacket, the cup set down on the table. I perform and record foley for your scenes, then edit every sound to the frame so it sits naturally in the mix. It is essential for animation, which has no production sound at all, and for films whose location sound needs to be rebuilt.

Everyday objects, the SCRATCH way

In my SCRATCH series, I rebuild well-known scenes entirely with everyday objects: chains, paper, glass, keys, a tape measure. In one episode, a hair dryer and a balloon became Shin Godzilla’s atomic breath. Performing sound with objects is the heart of that method, and of foley. Watch SCRATCH

What the foley includes

  • Footsteps, props and cloth, performed to your picture
  • Every sound edited and synced to the frame
  • Layered with sound design where the scene needs more
  • Delivered as separate tracks or mixed into your film (mixing)

Foley options

  • Half-day (4 hours)
  • Full day (8 hours)
  • How much picture fits in a session depends on how dense the scene is: you get an estimate with your quote

On short pieces, light foley is already part of the sound design price.

How a foley project works

  1. Send your picture and a short brief. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included unless stated otherwise.
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

Do I need to be there for the session?

No. You send the picture, I record and edit the foley, and you get the files by link.

Can you do foley for a game or an animation?

Yes. For animation, see sound for 3D animation; for games, game sound design.

Send the scene and tell me what it needs. You get a fixed quote and a delivery date.
