The Legend of Zelda chest sound, made from scratch

Opening a chest in Ocarina of Time is one of the most recognisable moments in gaming: the heavy lid, the pause, the little fanfare. For this SCRATCH episode, I muted the very first chest of the game, where Link finds the Kokiri Sword, and rebuilt both the sound and the music from nothing.

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the sound is made of

  • Chains
  • A stool
  • A piano, for the music
  • Everything recorded, then layered and processed in FL Studio

Each layer is shown on its own in the video, dry first, then with effects, so you can hear how a few everyday objects turn into one sound.

Why rebuild a sound everyone knows?

Because it shows what sound design really is. A game sound is rarely one recording: it is several layers, each doing one job, blended until they feel like a single object. The same method goes into client work, from sound effects for indie games to foley for film and animation.
