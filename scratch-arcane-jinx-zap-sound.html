Jinx’s Zap from Arcane, sound design made from scratch

In Arcane season 2, Jinx charges her laser gun, Zap, and fires. It lasts a few seconds on screen, but the sound behind it has to tell you everything: what the gun is made of, how much power it builds up and how hard it hits. For this SCRATCH episode, I muted the scene and rebuilt it with about ten layers.

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the sound is made of

  • Paper
  • Glass
  • A tape measure
  • Keys
  • Mechanics
  • An LFO synth
  • Electricity
  • The laser
  • The shot

All layered in FL Studio. Each layer appears on screen with its label, so you can hear the gun being assembled piece by piece.

A weapon sound is built, not found

No single recording sounds like a laser gun from an animated series. The everyday objects give it texture and detail, the synth and the electricity give it energy, and the shot gives it impact. That balance between real sources and design is what I bring to game sound design, ads and animation.
