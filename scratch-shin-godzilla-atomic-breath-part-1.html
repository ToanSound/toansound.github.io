Shin Godzilla’s atomic breath, made from scratch (Part 1)

In Shin Godzilla, the atomic breath is not fire: it is a thin purple beam that cuts through Tokyo, and the Shin Godzilla atomic breath sound is just as strange. For this episode, posted for the release of Godzilla Minus Zero, I muted the final beam scene and rebuilt it with three everyday objects. 820K+ views across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram (as of 23 November 2026).

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the sound is made of

  • A hair dryer
  • A balloon
  • A plastic water bottle

All layered in FL Studio, shown dry first, then with effects.

Turning a household noise into a weapon

A hair dryer already has the steady, airy power of a beam. Processed and layered with the balloon and the bottle, it becomes something you would never guess started as a hair dryer. Finding sources like this is what makes sound design feel unique, for films and video, games or foley.

The story continues in Part 2: the roar and the back plates charging up.
