Fiddlesticks from League of Legends, sound design made from scratch

Fiddlesticks is the scarecrow of League of Legends, and his champion trailer, "Terror in Demacia", plays like a horror film. For the very first SCRATCH episode, I muted the trailer and rebuilt its sound layer by layer, starting with the crows.

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the sound is made of

  • A crow impression, layered with reworked library crows
  • Forks
  • A voice
  • Synths played on the keys
  • Reworked library sounds

All layered in FL Studio. The names of the clips are visible on screen, so you can see exactly what each layer is.

Horror is built from small, wrong details

Fear in sound rarely comes from one big noise. It comes from details that feel slightly wrong: metal that scrapes, a voice that is almost human, birds that are too close. Mixing original recordings with reworked library sounds is how professional sound design works, from game sound design to sound design for film and video and foley.
