Sound design for 3D animation and renders

Your render looks great. Now make it sound great.

Got a silent animation, a product render or a motion design piece? I design the sound effects and ambience, sync every movement to picture and mix it all into a file ready to post. See Jinx’s Zap from Arcane, made from scratch. A one-line brief is enough to start.

  • Essential: up to 20 s, 1 round of revisions · typical turnaround: 5 working days
  • Standard: up to 40 s, 2 rounds of revisions, stems · typical turnaround: 5 working days

Ordering several renders at once? Ask for a bundle price.

Who it is for

3D artists, motion designers, animation students and studios, product and architecture visualisation. Animation has no production sound at all, so every sound is designed for it: movement, materials, ambience, the moment the camera lands.

What you send, what you get

Send the render (MP4 or MOV) and a line on the mood you want. You get a mixed file with sound, ready for your portfolio or social media, and a WAV of the sound track. The way you can use the sound is written in your quote (terms).

How a 3D animation sound project works

  1. Send your picture and a short brief. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included (one round for the Essential option).
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

Can you add music to my animation?

Yes. A short original loop can be added, see original music.

What if my animation has a lot of physical detail?

Footsteps, cloth and handled objects can be performed and recorded as foley for a more natural result.

Send your render and a line about the mood. You get a fixed price and a delivery date.
