The Godzilla roar, made from scratch with a balloon

The Godzilla roar is one of the most famous sounds in film history. When the trailer for Godzilla Minus Zero came out, I muted it and rebuilt the roar from nothing, with a balloon and my own voice. It became the most-watched SCRATCH episode: 1.4M+ views across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram (all posts of this video, as of 23 November 2026).

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the roar is made of

  • A deflating balloon
  • A rubbed balloon
  • My own scream, recorded into a mic
  • An aircraft whoosh, a library sound, twisted and reworked

All layered in FL Studio, shown dry first, then with effects.

A giant sound from tiny sources

A balloon is a few grams of rubber. Slowed down, pitched and layered with a voice, it becomes something enormous. That is the core of sound design: finding the right small source and shaping it until it carries the scale of the picture. I use the same approach for sound design for film and video, ads and game sound effects.
