Sound mixing for short films, video and podcasts

A good mix is the one nobody notices: every word is clear, the music carries the emotion and the effects hit without covering the dialogue. I mix short films, documentaries, ads and podcasts into a balanced stereo mix, checked for loudness and delivered to the spec of your platform or festival.

What the mix includes

  • Dialogue, music and effects balanced into one stereo mix
  • EQ, dynamics and reverb so every scene sounds coherent from shot to shot
  • Loudness measured for your delivery: web, festival or broadcast-style specs, confirmed with you at the brief
  • WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound; stems as an option
  • Two rounds of revisions

Dialogue editing and cleanup

If the production sound needs work first, I edit and clean the dialogue and ambiences before the mix: noise, hum, clicks, uneven levels, missing room tone.. Heavily damaged recordings are quoted after a listen.

How a mixing project works

  1. Send your picture and a short brief. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included unless stated otherwise.
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

What should I send for a film mix?

An AAF or OMF export from your editing software, or separate stems, plus a reference video. We confirm the exact format at the brief.

Do you mix in 5.1 or Dolby Atmos?

Not at the moment: I deliver stereo mixes.

Can you also add sound effects or foley?

Yes. Missing effects can be added with sound design, and footsteps, props and cloth with foley.

Send the film length, the delivery spec and your deadline, and you get a fixed quote in writing.
