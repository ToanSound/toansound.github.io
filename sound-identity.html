Sound logo design and sonic branding

Some brands are recognised in two seconds, eyes closed. A sound logo gives your brand, channel or app that kind of signature: short, memorable and consistent everywhere it plays, from an ad to a notification.

Sonic branding packages

  • Sound logo (3 to 8 s)
  • Podcast intro (10 to 15 s, plus outro)
  • App sounds (8 to 12 sounds)
  • Full sound identity (sound logo, 15 to 30 s jingle and 5 variations)

Typical turnaround: 7 to 10 working days. For the sound logo, the transfer of the rights you need to use it as your brand’s sound is included; its scope (media, territories, duration) is written in your quote. Trademark registration, if you want one, is handled on your side, and we cannot guarantee that a sound can be registered.

How a sound logo is made

  1. Brief: your brand, your audience and where the sound will play.
  2. Two directions: you hear two different ideas and pick one.
  3. Refinement: two rounds of revisions on the chosen direction.
  4. Delivery: the versions you need (short, long, with or without music) in formats for video, web and apps.

FAQ

What is the difference between a sound logo and a jingle?

A sound logo is a short signature of a few seconds, used at the end of an ad or when an app opens. A jingle is longer and more musical. The full identity package includes both.

Can you design the music for our ads too?

Yes, see original music and sound for ads.
