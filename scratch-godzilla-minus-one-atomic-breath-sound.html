Godzilla’s atomic breath over Ginza, made from scratch

The atomic breath over Ginza is the moment Godzilla Minus One turns into a disaster film: the dorsal fins light up, then the city explodes. To celebrate the release of Godzilla Minus Zero, I muted the scene and rebuilt it with three everyday sources. 680K+ views across TikTok, YouTube and Instagram (as of 27 November 2026).

The YouTube player loads only when you click (privacy).

What the sound is made of

  • A balloon, for the explosion
  • A zip, for the dorsal fins
  • A voice, for the roar

All layered in FL Studio, shown dry first, then with effects.

One job per layer

Each source does one thing: the zip builds tension, the voice gives the creature its presence, the balloon delivers the impact. Splitting a big moment into simple jobs is how I approach every sound design project, from trailers and ads to animation.
