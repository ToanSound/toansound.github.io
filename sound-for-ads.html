  • Sound design built for your spot, synced to picture

  • Options: stems, cutdowns and extra formats, quoted with the main spot

How an ad sound project works

  1. Send your picture and a short brief. A video export, a render or a build, plus a few lines on what you want (references welcome).
  2. Get a fixed quote in writing. Price, deadline and deliverables are agreed before any work starts.
  3. First version, then revisions. Two rounds of revisions are included unless stated otherwise.
  4. Final files, ready to use. WAV 24-bit / 48 kHz and an MP4 with sound, loudness-checked for where it will play.

FAQ

Yes: original music for the spot, or a sound logo the brand can reuse everywhere.
